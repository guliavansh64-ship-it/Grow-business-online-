Frequently asked questions

Answers about pricing, the refundable deposit, activation, orders, payouts and your storefront.

Getting started

A one-time ₹1,000 refundable security deposit, plus a 5% platform commission on delivered orders. There is no monthly fee and no setup charge.

Yes. It is a security deposit, not a fee. It is returned in full when you close your store in good standing — no pending disputes, no unpaid dues.

Activation starts the moment your deposit is confirmed and finishes within 48 hours. You can add products and branding while you wait.

No. You get a storefront on your own subdomain, and everything is managed from a dashboard. If you can use WhatsApp, you can manage your store.

Orders & payments

UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards, net banking, and cash on delivery. You can switch COD off for your store.

An order settles to your available balance the moment you mark it delivered. Withdrawals need a minimum of ₹500 and are usually paid within 1–2 working days.

On a ₹1,000 delivered order, the platform keeps ₹50 and you receive ₹950. Cancelled and refunded orders are never charged. The calculation always happens on the server.

Yes. Open the order in your dashboard, choose Refund and add a reason. Stock is restored and the commission is reversed automatically.

Your store

Your store starts on yourname.growbusinessonline.com. A custom domain can be mapped later by pointing a CNAME at your storefront.

As many as you like. Categories, search, filters and pagination keep large catalogues fast for shoppers.

Yes, from Store Settings. Changes go live immediately and are reflected on your storefront, favicon and social share cards.

Every storefront is mobile-first and tested from 320px phones up to 1440px desktops, with touch-friendly buttons and no horizontal scrolling.

Account & security

Passwords are hashed, sessions are signed and revocable, every request is re-authorised on the server, and one store can never read another store’s data.

Yes. Accounts have roles — vendor, staff, customer and platform admin — so you can give limited access without sharing your password.

Use “Forgot password” on the login page. A reset link is emailed to you and, for safety, it signs out every other device.

Your storefront goes offline, your refundable deposit is returned, and your order history stays available for accounting and GST purposes.
