Vendor Agreement

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The specific terms between GrowBusiness Online and every store operating on the platform.

1. Relationship

You operate as an independent business. Nothing here creates employment, partnership or agency. You are the seller of record for your products.

2. Store activation

Your store is activated within 48 hours of a confirmed security deposit. Activation may take longer if business details need verification, and we will tell you why.

3. Listing standards

Product titles, images and descriptions must be accurate and your own. Prices must include applicable taxes unless clearly stated otherwise. You may not list prohibited or counterfeit items.

4. Order fulfilment

Confirm or cancel a new order within 24 hours. Ship within the handling time you publish, upload tracking where available, and keep stock levels accurate.

5. Commission and settlement

The platform commission is 5% of the order value on delivered orders. Settlement happens automatically on delivery, and the split is visible on every order and in your ledger.

6. Withdrawals

Minimum withdrawal is ₹500 from your available balance. Requests are reviewed for fraud and compliance, and payouts are normally made within 1–2 working days of approval.

7. Taxes and invoices

You are responsible for GST registration, invoicing customers where required, filing returns and paying taxes on your income. The platform issues a tax invoice for the commission it charges.

8. Customer data

Customer details shared with you are for fulfilling that order only. You may not use them for unrelated marketing, share them, or sell them.

9. Branding and conduct

Your storefront must not display content that is unlawful, hateful, obscene or misleading, and must not impersonate another brand or the platform itself.

10. Support standards

Respond to customer messages within one working day. Repeated failure to respond is grounds for suspension.

11. Suspension

We may suspend a store immediately for fraud, prohibited products, repeated policy breaches or a legal requirement. You will be notified with the reason and how to appeal.

12. Termination and deposit refund

Either party may end this agreement with 30 days notice. On closure in good standing your refundable deposit is returned within 15 working days, less any amounts owed to customers or the platform.

13. Dispute resolution

Disputes are first resolved by discussion, then by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at Jhajjar, Haryana. Courts at Jhajjar have exclusive jurisdiction.
